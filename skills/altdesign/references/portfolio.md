## 2. Cross-cutting patterns

Split by type: 13 of 19 are heroes (w02, w05, w06, w08, w09, w11, w12, w13, w14, w15, w16, w17, w18), 2 are app dashboards (w03, w04), 3 are feature sections (w07, w10, w19), 1 is a footer (w01).

### Hero layout
- Left aligned text with the picture on the right or behind: 7 of 13 heroes (w02, w12, w13, w14, w16, w17, w18).
- Centred stack: 5 of 13 (w05, w06, w08, w11, w15).
- Split heading left, subhead plus CTAs right: 1 of 13 (w09).
- The text column in left aligned heroes takes 33% to 40% of the width (w02 33%, w13 36%, w14 36%, w12 40%, w16 39%, w17 38%, w18 39%). Centred columns run 35% to 48% of the width (about 500 to 690 px at 1440).
- The picture is full bleed behind the text in 9 of 13 (w02, w08, w09, w11, w12, w13, w15, w16, w17). Only w14 and w18 use a boxed or isolated object on a plain field, and only w05 and w06 have no main picture.
- A product UI or code peeks from the bottom edge of the hero in 4 of 13 (w06, w08, w13 app windows; w17 code card).

### Headline length and line breaks
- 10 of 13 headings are exactly 2 lines; 2 are 3 lines (w05, w18); 1 is a single line (w02).
- Word counts: 4, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 8, 10, 12. Median 7 words. 9 of 13 are 8 words or fewer.
- Lines break on phrase boundaries, by hand: "Real agents, / real control.", "Travel farther / Experience more", "An Army of AI / One Powerful Vision". 5 of 13 are two parallel halves (w09, w12, w15, w16, w17 shape).
- Heading size is about 48 to 64 px at 1440 in 12 of 13. None is giant (no 96 px+ type). The heading takes 3% to 8% of frame height per line.
- Case: sentence case 7 (w02, w05, w06, w12, w13, w14, w17), Title Case 6 (w08, w09, w11, w15 per line, w16, w18).

### Type
- Sans headings: 15 of 19. Serif headings: 3 (w06, w12, w16). Pixel or dot-matrix display: 1 (w18). Monospace used as an accent eyebrow: 1 (w16).
- When the heading is serif, the body is also serif in 2 of 3 (w06, w16); w12 pairs serif with a sans body.
- Weight: bold or extra bold 7 (w05, w06, w08, w11, w14, w15, w09 semibold); regular or medium 6 (w12, w13, w16, w17, w18, w07/w10 sections). Light weights only in data figures (w02, w04).
- Tracking is tight (about -0.02 to -0.04 em) on every sans heading; never letter-spaced, never all caps. Uppercase letter-spaced text appears only as tiny meta lines in UIs (w03).
- Two tone headings (some words in grey or a lighter tint): 4 of 13 heroes (w05, w09 per-word gradient, w12 fade at the end, w14). A white to grey gradient on dark: only w12 (and faintly w11).

### Colour strategy
- Exactly one accent colour per page in 17 of 19. Accents: orange or red-orange 5 (w05, w07, w08, w14, plus the w13 dot), green 3 (w06, w17, w18 mint), blue 3 (w10, w19, w09 navy), rust 1 (w12). w02 and w04 use multicolour only inside data (chart series, traffic light).
- Monochrome or single hue page (art, type and buttons all from one hue): 4 (w01 blue ink, w09 blue ink, w16 crimson, w17 green). Strongly two hue (dark field plus one warm accent in the art): w12 navy plus rust, w13 black plus gold.
- Light vs dark: dark 9 (w04, w07, w10, w12, w13, w15, w16, w17, w18), light 7 (w01, w03, w05, w06, w09, w14, w19), mid-tone colour fields 3 (w02 blue sky, w08 teal, w11 warm gradient).
- Light canvases are never pure white: #F4F4F4, #F5F5F5, #F6F5F4, #F5E9D7 cream, #DCD4CC paper. Dark canvases are near black or a deep hue: #000, #11110F, #252525, #122939 navy, #67191F crimson, #0A1819.

## 3. What separates this from generic AI-made landing pages

1. **One scene, one pun.** The hero picture is a single scene that acts out the headline (red light and "stop spending", frog and "catch every bug", robot chef and "let Claude cook"). Check: can you say in 5 words how the picture shows the headline? Shown in w02, w08, w09, w12, w13, w14, w15, w16, w17, w18 (10 of 13 heroes).
2. **Pick a medium, not a gradient.** The art has a named craft style (etching, anime sky, watercolour, pixel art, halftone print, dot-matrix). Ban blurred blobs, glowing orbs and abstract mesh gradients as the main visual. Check: the art style can be named in two words. w01, w02, w09, w12, w14, w17, w18. Only w11 uses a blurred backdrop, and it compensates with a prompt box.
3. **Run the hue through everything.** Pick the art first, then pull the canvas, type tint, button and logo colours from it, with exactly one accent. Check: count distinct hues outside charts; 2 or fewer. w01, w09, w12, w16, w17 are single hue; 17 of 19 have one accent.
4. **Short, parallel, two line headings.** 4 to 8 words, broken by hand at a phrase, often two parallel halves. Check: 2 lines, 8 words or fewer, no orphan word. 10 of 13 are 2 lines, median 7 words (w12, w15, w16, w17, w09).
5. **Headline at 48 to 64 px, not 96.** Let the picture carry the scale. Tight tracking, no all caps. Check: heading font size at 1440 px is between 44 and 68 px. 12 of 13 heroes.
6. **Make the product the decoration when there is no scene.** Use real-looking UI cards (booking cards, profile cards, tables, charts) as moving ornaments, not icons in circles. Check: no feature card uses a lone icon as its visual. w05, w07, w10, w19 (0 icon-only cards in the portfolio).
7. **Let UI bleed and crop.** App windows peek from the bottom of the hero; card visuals are cut by the card edge. Check: at least one mockup crosses a container edge. w06, w08, w13, w17 (hero peeks); w03, w07, w10, w19 (cropped cards).
8. **Every animation shows the product working.** Counters tick, text types, cursors hover rows, bars grow, a dashboard paints in. Check: name the product fact each animation shows; if none, cut it. 7 of 8 animated works (w03, w04, w05, w07, w10, w16, w19); w08 is the one ambient exception.
9. **Off-white and off-black, never #FFF or a generic slate.** Light canvases sit at #F4F4F4 to #F6F5F4 or a cream; dark ones are near black or a deep hue (navy #122939, crimson #67191F). Check: canvas hex is not #FFFFFF and not a Tailwind slate. All 19.
10. **Pick an unexpected type voice once.** A serif on an AI product (w06, w12, w16), a dot-matrix font on a keyboard (w18), a monospace eyebrow (w16), or two tone grey words (w05, w14). Check: the heading has one deliberate non-default choice. 8 of 13 heroes.
11. **Proof inside the fold, quiet.** One proof element (monochrome logos, stats row with hairline dividers, stars, Product Hunt badge) directly under the CTAs or at the bottom edge of the hero. Check: proof visible without scrolling and in one colour. 9 of 13.
12. **Buttons carry an icon and a hierarchy.** Primary filled (often with a chevron in a circle or an arrow, sometimes a soft sheen), secondary a quieter white, outline or play icon. Never two equal buttons. Check: primary and secondary differ in fill. All 8 two-CTA heroes.

## 4. Contradictions with, or extensions of, the designer's own skill rules

- **Hero heading gradient white to #9B9B9B on dark.** Rare in the work. Of 9 dark works, only w12 has a visible fade to grey at the end of line 2 (and w11 a faint warm translucence on a mid-tone field). w08, w13, w15, w16, w17, w18 use flat white or off-white. On light pages the designer instead uses two tone words (w05, w14: black plus grey #5E5E5E to #6B6B6B) or a per-word hue gradient (w09 navy to slate). So the rule generalises to "lower the contrast of part of the heading", not a fixed gradient.
- **Heading and subheading max 680 px.** Holds for centred heroes (w05 about 680 px, w11 about 680 px, w06 about 610 px, w08 about 620 px). Left aligned heroes run 33% to 40% of the frame, which is about 480 to 580 px at 1440, so they are narrower than 680. The w05 heading is 3 lines at 680 px, so the limit drives line count, not just width.
- **Floating blurred nav.** Contradicted by most heroes. Only 4 of 13 use a floating container (w05, w06, w13 solid, w08 the only clearly blurred glass). 8 of 13 use a flush transparent bar over the picture. The pattern seems to be: floating pill on plain or UI-led pages (w05, w06), flush bar when a full-bleed picture fills the hero (w09, w12, w15, w16, w17, w18) so the nav does not cover the art.
- **Scroll reveals 32 px rise, 600 to 800 ms, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1).** The GIFs do not show any rise-and-fade on scroll. The load-ins shown are different: blur-to-sharp stagger (w03), opacity plus tint lift (w08), a panel paint-in (w04), fade from blank (w19). Most motion is in-content looping (marquee, counters, typing, cursor, marching dashes, ASCII morph). The skill should add: count-up numbers, blur-in staggers, vertical card marquees with edge fades, and an image-to-image ASCII dither transition (w16).
- **Mandatory tagline section where words brighten one by one.** Not visible in any of the 19 shots (they show heroes, feature cards, dashboards and a footer). It cannot be confirmed or denied from these images.
- **Spacing scale 2/4/8/12/16/24/32/40/48/64/80/96.** Consistent with what can be measured: card gaps about 12 px (w10, w19), card padding about 16 to 24 px (w03, w07, w19), eyebrow to heading about 24 px, CTA gaps about 8 to 12 px, section heading to cards about 48 to 64 px (w07, w10). No contradiction found.
- **Beyond the skill: imagery is the core move, and the skill rules above say nothing about it.** 11 of 19 works are carried by one commissioned-looking AI picture in a named craft style that puns on the headline. That is the biggest gap between the stated rules and the portfolio.
- **Beyond the skill: dark is not the default.** 7 of 19 are light, 3 are mid-tone colour fields. The skill's dark-first rules (white to grey gradient) would not produce w05, w06, w09, w14 or w19.
- **Beyond the skill: serif and novelty display type.** 3 serif headings and 1 dot-matrix heading; the skill rules imply a single sans heading style.
- **Beyond the skill: button details.** Chevron in a circle inside the primary (w05, w14, w17), soft vertical sheen on accent buttons, coloured glow under the primary (w05, w14), play icon secondary (w08, w12), price in the button (w18).
